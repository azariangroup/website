Garage Conversion ADU

Converting a garage into living space means adapting an existing structure to residential use. This profile outlines common scope items; existing conditions, code requirements and approval pathways need to be confirmed for each property.

Project overview

Planning a garage conversion project

A conversion may require evaluation of the slab, framing, ceiling height, moisture control, insulation, egress, fire separation and utility connections. The finished layout can combine sleeping, living, cooking and bathing areas within a compact footprint.

Typical scope considerations

  • Existing structure and slab assessment
  • Insulation, moisture and fire-separation details
  • Window, door and egress planning
  • Electrical, plumbing and interior finish work

A coordinated construction process

Assess existing conditions

Document the garage structure and services to identify upgrades that may be needed for the proposed use.

Design a practical layout

Coordinate natural light, privacy, storage, bathroom placement and efficient circulation.

Complete the conversion

Carry out approved structural and building-system upgrades, then finish and inspect the new living space.
