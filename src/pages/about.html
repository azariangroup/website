Our Story

A better way to build

Azarian Inc began with a belief that the construction experience could be better — more organized, more honest and more personal. That belief still drives every decision we make.

Our team of project managers, carpenters and vetted trade partners delivers homes, additions, ADUs, remodels and commercial spaces throughout Los Angeles County and Orange County, with one point of contact from estimate to final walkthrough.

We are licensed, bonded and insured in the State of California, and we approach every project, large or small, with the same attention to detail.

Our Process

Five steps to a successful build

A proven, repeatable framework that keeps quality high and surprises low.

  1. 01

    Discovery Visit

    We review your goals, site and budget, and outline what is possible.

  2. 02

    Design & Estimate

    Plans are drawn and engineered while we build a detailed cost breakdown.

  3. 03

    Permitting

    We prepare submittals and manage plan check until approvals are issued.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Your project manager leads the work and keeps you informed weekly.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Final walkthrough, punch list completion and warranty documentation.
