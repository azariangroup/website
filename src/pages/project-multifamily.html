Small Multifamily Construction

Small multifamily projects need coordinated planning for the site, shared systems and individual units. This representative profile describes considerations for a duplex, fourplex or small apartment building without implying a particular completed project.

Project overview

Planning a multifamily construction project

A multifamily scope can involve early site and utility review, coordination of architectural and structural plans, phased construction, and consistent quality checks across units and common areas. Final requirements depend on the approved plans and jurisdiction.

Typical scope considerations

  • Site and utility coordination
  • Foundations, framing and building envelope
  • Repeatable unit layouts and shared systems
  • Common areas, inspections and closeout

A coordinated construction process

Review feasibility

Coordinate the proposed unit count, access, parking, utilities and consultant drawings for the property.

Sequence shared work

Plan foundations, structure and building systems so shared work and unit-level work remain coordinated.

Inspect by phase

Track inspections, finish consistency and closeout details across units and common areas.
