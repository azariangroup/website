Commercial Tenant Improvements

Tenant improvements adapt an existing commercial space for a new occupant or use. This representative profile describes common coordination needs; lease terms, building conditions, design and jurisdictional review shape each project.

Project overview

Planning a commercial construction project

Commercial interior work may include selective demolition, partitions, ceilings, lighting, power, HVAC distribution, plumbing, life-safety coordination, accessibility upgrades and finish installation. Building rules and operating hours can affect sequencing.

Typical scope considerations

  • Existing-space and landlord requirement review
  • Partition, ceiling and finish coordination
  • Electrical, HVAC and life-safety interfaces
  • Phasing, inspections and turnover planning

A coordinated construction process

Confirm building constraints

Review existing conditions, access, building rules, utility capacity and the proposed use.

Coordinate design and approvals

Align the tenant scope with consultants, landlord requirements and applicable plan review.

Build and prepare for turnover

Sequence work around site rules, track inspections and complete agreed closeout items.
