Bathroom Remodeling

Bathroom renovation requires careful coordination of waterproofing, plumbing fixtures, ventilation, lighting and tile. This representative profile outlines typical considerations; exact scope depends on the existing room and selected design.

Project overview

Planning a bathroom remodeling project

A bathroom project may retain the existing layout or relocate fixtures. Work can include demolition, plumbing and electrical updates, waterproofing, shower or tub installation, tile, cabinetry, ventilation and finish hardware.

Typical scope considerations

  • Fixture layout and plumbing review
  • Waterproofing and shower assembly
  • Tile, vanity, lighting and hardware selection
  • Ventilation and finish coordination

A coordinated construction process

Review the room

Confirm measurements, existing plumbing, ventilation and any concealed-condition concerns before setting scope.

Coordinate critical details

Select fixtures and tile, align rough-in locations and plan waterproofing transitions.

Install and inspect

Complete rough-in work, wet-area assemblies and finishes, then check operation and finish details.
