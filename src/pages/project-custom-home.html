Modern Custom Home Construction

A ground-up home starts with the property itself. Site access, grading, utilities, structural design and permitting all shape the plan. This profile outlines a representative custom-home scope for Southern California homeowners.

Project overview

Planning a custom home construction project

A custom home project brings the design team, construction plan and property requirements together before work begins. Scope can include preconstruction coordination, site preparation, foundations, framing, building systems, interior finishes and final inspections.

Typical scope considerations

  • Site feasibility and preconstruction coordination
  • Grading, foundations and structural framing
  • Electrical, plumbing and HVAC coordination
  • Interior finish installation and closeout

A coordinated construction process

Plan around the site

Review access, existing utilities, topography, design documents and permit requirements before establishing a build sequence.

Coordinate the build

Schedule trades and inspections across the major phases, with decisions and material selections tracked against the approved scope.

Complete and close out

Finish interior and exterior details, address inspection items and document closeout requirements.
