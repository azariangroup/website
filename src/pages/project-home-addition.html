Home Additions and Second-Story Expansions

A home addition can expand a living area, add a bedroom or create a second story. This representative profile explains common construction considerations; the property, design and local review process determine what is feasible.

Project overview

Planning a residential addition project

Addition work ties new construction into an existing home. Planning can include foundation capacity, structural connections, roof and exterior transitions, utility upgrades, access, temporary weather protection and consistency between old and new finishes.

Typical scope considerations

  • Existing structure and foundation review
  • Architectural and structural coordination
  • Roof, exterior and utility tie-ins
  • Interior finish transitions and inspections

A coordinated construction process

Evaluate the home

Review the existing structure, site constraints, desired space and likely utility or access impacts.

Coordinate the connection

Plan structural, roofing, waterproofing and mechanical details where new and existing construction meet.

Build and integrate

Sequence the addition with temporary protection and finish work that connects the new area to the home.
