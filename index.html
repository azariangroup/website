How It Works

A clear path from idea to move-in

Our five-stage process removes guesswork, so you always know what is happening, what comes next and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Discovery Visit

    We walk the site, listen to your goals and talk candidly about budget, feasibility and timing.

  2. 02

    Design & Estimate

    Our architects and engineers develop plans while we prepare a transparent, line-item estimate.

  3. 03

    Permitting

    We assemble the submittal, respond to plan-check comments and secure your building permits.

  4. 04

    Construction

    Your project manager runs the jobsite, coordinates inspections and sends weekly progress updates.

  5. 05

    Handover

    We complete a detailed walkthrough, finish every punch item and hand over warranties and documents.

Ask Us Anything
How much does it cost to build an ADU or home addition in Los Angeles?

Every site is different, so pricing depends on size, design, finishes, utilities and site conditions. After a free on-site visit we provide a detailed written estimate with line items, so you know exactly where your budget goes before any work begins.

Do you take care of plans and building permits?

Yes. We coordinate architects, structural engineers and Title 24 consultants, prepare the submittal package and manage plan check with the city or county until permits are issued.

How long will my project take?

Kitchen and bath remodels typically take 4–10 weeks of construction, ADUs and additions 4–8 months, and custom homes 12–18 months. Permitting time varies by jurisdiction. You receive a written schedule before we start.

Are you licensed and insured?

Azarian Inc is a California licensed general contractor carrying general liability, workers' compensation and a contractor's bond. Certificates are available on request for every project.

Which areas do you serve?

We work throughout Los Angeles County, including the San Fernando Valley, Glendale, Burbank, Pasadena, the Westside and surrounding communities.
